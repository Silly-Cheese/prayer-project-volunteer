Training

Prepare before serving.

This training introduces the heart of the volunteer program, privacy expectations, chapter standards, and care project practices.

1. Mission

Volunteers exist to support prayer, encouragement, and care without drawing attention away from the people being served.

2. Privacy

Prayer requests should be handled with restraint. Do not share details outside approved systems.

3. Chapters

Chapters must have a clear purpose, safe leadership, and consistent reporting.

Training Completion

Accepted volunteers can mark training completed inside their portal. Admins may still review and set final status to Active.
