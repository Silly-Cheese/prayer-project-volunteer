Volunteer Network

Organized prayer, encouragement, and practical service.

The volunteer side of The Prayer Project exists to move beyond a simple prayer wall into trained service, local and online chapters, care projects, accountability, and meaningful encouragement.

Mission

Faithful care with structure.

Volunteers serve with compassion, privacy, training, documentation, and clear leadership so care never becomes careless.

Prayer Support

Pray over requests, support weekly prayer focuses, and help keep the project spiritually centered.

Volunteer Chapters

Build school, church, local, or online chapters with approved leadership and clear standards.

Care Projects

Plan encouragement campaigns, service ideas, awareness efforts, and acts of practical care.

Volunteer Path

A cleaner process from application to service.

Applicants apply, get reviewed, receive a one-time activation code if accepted, complete onboarding, accept the handbook standards, and then begin service through their assigned team and chapter.

1. Apply to volunteer.
2. Wait for admin review.
3. Activate your account with a one-time code.
4. Complete training and accept the agreement.
5. Begin assignments, service logs, chapters, care projects, and suggestions.

Service Logs

Document time served so leadership can track real impact and volunteer consistency.

Assignments

Receive tasks from leadership, complete them, and keep volunteer work organized.

Suggestions

Submit ideas for chapters, policies, projects, training, outreach, and website improvements.
