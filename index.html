Volunteer Network

Serve people through prayer, encouragement, and local care.

A professional volunteer home for people who want to help The Prayer Project grow beyond a prayer wall into chapters, care projects, outreach, and organized service.

Purpose

Quiet service. Real people. Faithful care.

Volunteers help pray, encourage, build chapters, and support real-world acts of care while respecting privacy, dignity, and the heart of the mission.

Prayer Support Team

Volunteers pray over requests, submit encouragement updates, and help keep the mission spiritually centered.

Chapter Leadership

Accepted volunteers can request chapters for schools, churches, cities, online groups, or community service teams.

Care Projects

Volunteers can help organize cards, encouragement campaigns, local service ideas, awareness projects, and practical acts of care.

How it works

Apply, get reviewed, then activate your volunteer account.

After an application is accepted, an admin generates a user-specific temporary code. That code can be used once to create your login, role, team, status, and permissions profile.

1. Submit a volunteer application.
2. An admin reviews your application.
3. If accepted, you receive a one-time temporary code.
4. Activate your account and access the volunteer portal.
